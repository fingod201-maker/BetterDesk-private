Privacy Policy

Zoom Private — remote support and unattended access

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zoom Private client application ("the App") and the associated landing/download service ("the Service") handle information. The App is provided to you by the organization that issued your connection details ("your Operator"). Your Operator is the data controller for the remote-support sessions you take part in; the App itself does not maintain a user account or advertising profile.

1. Information the App processes

  • Connection details — operator host, port and (optionally) a pre-configured access credential, used to reach your Operator.
  • Device information — device model, operating-system version, screen size and a generated device identifier, used to establish and display a remote session.
  • Screen content — when you start screen sharing, the screen is captured and transmitted to your Operator for the duration of the session. Capture stops when the session ends or you turn it off.
  • Remote input — when you enable the Accessibility service, an authorized Operator may send touch gestures (taps, swipes) to your device while connected.
  • Diagnostic logs — limited technical logs (for example, a crash log) kept on your device to help diagnose failures. These stay on the device unless you or your Operator retrieve them.

2. Accessibility service

The App offers an optional Android Accessibility service so an authorized Operator can control your device during a remote-support session. The App shows a prominent disclosure and asks for your consent before you enable it, and it is used only for remote support and unattended access. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

3. How information is used

Information is processed solely to provide the remote-support and unattended-access functionality: establishing sessions, transmitting your screen and input, keeping the connection alive, and diagnosing faults. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Sharing

Session data is transmitted to the Operator you connect to. Download requests to the Service are served from a hosting provider and may be logged by that provider for security and reliability. We do not otherwise disclose information except where required by law.

5. Retention

Live screen and input data are transient and are not stored by the App after a session ends. On-device diagnostic logs remain until you clear them or uninstall the App. Your Operator's retention of session data is governed by your Operator's own policy.

6. Security

Connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS/WSS) where configured. Access to a device requires the credential set by your Operator.

7. Children

The App is a workplace/technical tool and is not directed to children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact your Operator using the support contact they provided, or email privacy@telcom.stream.
