# BetterDesk — Landing Portal Launch (zoom.telcom.stream)

**Channel:** #it-announcements / #all-staff  
**Owner:** IT Operations  
**Status:** Live

🚀 *New remote access portal is now available*

Visit: **https://zoom.telcom.stream**

## What it is
Single-page portal to download the BetterDesk Agent for your device. Auto-detects Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android — one click to get the right installer.

## Admin console
**https://panel.telcom.stream** — IT staff can view online devices and start browser-based remote-control sessions.

## How to install
1. Open **https://zoom.telcom.stream** on your device.
2. Download the detected installer.
3. Run it — enrolled devices auto-connect to the org.

## For IT / Operators
Log in at **https://panel.telcom.stream** → select device → click **Remote**. Sessions are consent-gated unless unattended policy is set.

Questions? Ping #it-help or email ops@telcom.stream.
